## 1. Baseline: playable game, five content packs

**What**
- Vite + vanilla TypeScript app with no UI framework, built to static files.
- The canvas pixel-art scene (`src/render/scene.ts`) has a torch-lit shaft, a bonfire with a
  coiled sword, ash/ember particles, banded pixel lighting, a knight that sinks
  as you score, and red eyes deeper down.
- Synthesized WebAudio sound effects (`src/render/sfx.ts`).
- Screens:
  - home (bonfire)
  - Daily Descent
  - Themed Catacombs (4 themes + 3 sealed)
  - Archive (practice)
  - game
  - results with emoji share
  - how-to-play and settings modals
- i18n for EN, PL and UK, auto-detected and switchable.
- The answer engine (`src/engine/normalize.ts`, `match.ts`):
  - Cyrillic transliteration, diacritic stripping and phonetic folding
  - Roman numerals, typo tolerance by length, and a per-prompt reject list
  - exact spelling takes priority, which keeps кіт (cat) and кит (whale) apart
- Daily deal: a weighted rotation across packs with seeded per-pack decks, so no repeat
  within 9 days. A run in progress is pinned to its prompt ids, and reloading
  counts the current prompt as lost.
- Content written by agents and checked by `npm run validate`:

| geography | 32 | 1,523 |
| movies | 26 | 1,493 |

| videogames | 26 | 1,918 |

**Verified:** `npm run build` is clean and the validator reports 0 errors. Playwright (Firefox) runs covered:

## 2. Difficulty tags on every prompt + content guide

**What**
- New required field `d: 1 | 2 | 3` (easy/medium/hard) on `PromptSpec`. It measures
  how hard it is to name *any* valid answer for a general player.
- All 144 existing prompts are tagged by hand (`scripts/tag-difficulty.py`, kept for
  reference). Split: 50 easy / 74 medium / 20 hard, so new content should lean
  toward hard.
- The geography country prompts are generated by `countries()`, so their tags live in
  `COUNTRY_DIFFICULTY`.
- The validator now requires `d` and warns when a question is longer than 60 characters.
- `docs/content-guide.md` is the single brief for anyone (human or agent) writing
  packs: schema, tiers, difficulty, wording rules, validation.

**Verified:** the validator reports 0 errors and `tsc` is clean.

## 3. Difficulty in play: easy → hard descent, skull badges

## 4. Dark Souls easter eggs, depth zones

All of this is flavour; none of it changes scoring. It lives in `src/eggs.ts`.

**Depth zones.** Crossing a depth threshold shows a DS-style area banner
(thin rules above and below, centred over the knight). It also eases the shaft into
that zone's look:
- a colour wash over the stone (canvas `color` blend, so shading is kept)
- the darkness level
- the colour of the drifting motes

| From | Zone | Look |
|---:|---|---|
| 0 m | The Bonfire Shaft | neutral |
| 600 m | Undead Burrows | dusty |
| 1,500 m | The Depths | teal, damp |
| 2,500 m | Blightpit | sickly green, green spores |
| 3,500 m | Catacombs of the Hollow | bone |
| 4,500 m | Tomb of Giants | near-black |
| 5,500 m | The Abyss | violet, violet motes |
| 6,500 m | Kiln of the First Flame | pale ash, white ash fall |

Zone names are translated into PL and UK.

**Secret phrases.** These are typed into the answer box and never consume the turn,
unless the phrase is somehow a valid answer:
- *praise the sun* / `\[T]/` / *chwała słońcu* / *слава сонцю*: the knight
  strikes the \[T]/ pose (new `KNIGHT_PRAISE` sprite), with a golden spark ring, a
  chord, and a "PRAISE THE SUN!" banner.
- *git gud* and *rest at bonfire* get a one-line reply.

**Death quips.** Offering a famous DS name as a wrong answer replaces "Not
inscribed in the tome":
- Solaire: "If only you could be so grossly incandescent."
- Siegmeyer is napping, and Patches apologises.
- Artorias, Gwyn, Ornstein/Smough, Estus, Fire Keeper, Mimic and "you died" each have a line.
- Any other wrong answer has a 4% chance of "Patches kicked you down the shaft".

**Hollowing.** After three deaths in one run, the knight's armour rusts
(`HOLLOW_PALETTE` recolour).

**BONFIRE LIT.** Starting a fresh Daily Descent kindles the bonfire with the
golden banner and a whoosh. The ask panel stays hidden until it fades.

**Endings.** 600+ points gives "VICTORY ACHIEVED" instead of "DESCENT COMPLETE".
Seven deaths gives a red "YOU DIED".

**At the bonfire (home):**
- An orange soapstone rune glows on the floor. Tapping it reads a message in the
  series' style ("Try platypus", "Rare answer ahead", "Fort, night"…, 16 of them, in all three
  languages) with a random "Appraised ×N".
- Tapping the title 5×, 10×… makes the Fire Keeper speak.
- The Konami code (↑↑↓↓←→←→BA) makes the seated knight stand up and praise the sun.

**Also fixed**
- The bonfire floor now vanishes quickly when a descent starts. It used to hang in the air
  for a moment.
- The scene is exposed as `window.scene` in dev builds only, for testing.

**Verified:** Playwright screenshots of each egg and of all eight zones (forced
depth through the dev hook). `tsc` and the build are clean.

## 6. New mode: Endless Abyss (Estus flasks as lives)

**What**
- **Endless Abyss** (`#/endless`) is a new home card under the Daily Descent.
  - You get three Estus flasks. Each wrong answer or timeout drains one; when the last is gone the run ends.
  - The depth is unbounded.
- Prompts come from every pack, with difficulty rising over the run:
  - the first 3 are easy
  - then mostly medium
  - from the 15th onward, mostly hard
  - each difficulty deals from its own deck, so nothing repeats until that deck is exhausted
- HUD: three pixel Estus flasks replace the 7 pips, and they grey out with a little
  pop when drained. The kicker shows "Prompt N" without a total.
- Results show "N prompts faced" instead of "of 7,000 m", and "Descend again" restarts.
  The best depth and play count are stored like a pack (`packs.endless`), and the home card shows the
  best depth.
- New deepest zone, only reachable in Endless: **The Age of Dark** at 9,000 m. It's
  pitch black, with pale motes.
- New pixel icon: an Estus flask.
- Wording: Themed Catacombs' subtitle is now "Seven prompts from a single theme",
  to avoid confusion with Endless.

**Verified:** played three deaths in Endless (flasks drain → results), plus home
card screenshots. `tsc` and the build are clean.

## 8. Offline play (service worker) + idle pack prefetch

**What**
- `public/sw.js` is a small hand-written service worker with no library:
  - hashed `/assets/*` are served cache-first, since they never change
  - pages are network-first with a cache fallback, so new deploys show up immediately
  - old caches are deleted on activate
- It is registered only in production builds.
- After first paint, every content pack is loaded in `requestIdleCallback`. The daily
  starts instantly, and the service worker has everything cached for offline play.

**Verified:** Playwright with the context switched offline after one online
visit. `#/daily` still renders its first prompt. `tsc` and the build are clean.

## 10. Audio only after a gesture; resilient content loading

**What**
- Audio: no `AudioContext` is created until the first pointer or key gesture, and it is
  created inside that gesture. This removes the browser's autoplay warnings, for example on
  a first visit when How-to-play opens automatically and its click sound fired.
- Content loading:
  - A pack chunk that fails to load (offline, or a tab left open across a deploy so its
    hashed chunks are gone) used to stay cached as a rejected promise, so that pack could
    never load again in that tab. Failed loads are now evicted, so they retry.
  - The idle prefetch swallows its errors.
  - If a screen can't be built, the router shows "The path is blocked. The
    tome could not be fetched." with a **Rekindle (reload)** button (EN/PL/UK),
    instead of silently staying on the old screen.

**Verified:**
- Playwright with no service worker and the sports chunk blocked: the error screen
  appears, and after unblocking, Rekindle loads the prompt.
- A sound-on run shows no autoplay warnings.
